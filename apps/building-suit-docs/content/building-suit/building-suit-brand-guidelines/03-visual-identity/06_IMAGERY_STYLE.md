# Imagery Style

> **Status:** Complete (initial). The photography, illustration, and abstract-visual direction for Building Suit. Imagery should feel premium, warm, and trustworthy — buildings and the communities inside them, lit and well-kept.

---

## Photography direction
**Mood:** premium, warm, calm, real. Buildings and people that feel cared-for and genuine — not stocky or staged.

- **Lighting:** warm, natural light — golden-hour tones, lit windows at dusk (echo the logo's "lights on"). Soft, directional, never harsh or flat.
- **Color treatment:** lean into navy/blue shadows and warm gold highlights; keep a cohesive, slightly desaturated-yet-rich grade. A subtle navy duotone or warm-cool split-tone is on-brand for hero crops.
- **Composition:** clean, architectural framing — strong verticals/horizontals, generous negative space for text overlay, clear focal point.
- **Subjects:** residential buildings (façades, entrances, lit windows), residents and admins in everyday building life, neighbourly moments.
- **Authenticity:** real, diverse, regionally appropriate people and buildings; candid over posed.
- **Overlay:** when placing text, use a navy scrim/gradient for contrast; keep the logo in clear space (see logo rules).

## Illustration direction
Use illustration for explanation, empty states, onboarding, and friendly moments.

- **Style:** clean, geometric, line-led with flat fills and **subtle depth** (light shadows), consistent with the icon style and the logo's geometry.
- **Palette:** navy + neutrals as the base, **gold as the single accent**; semantic colors only where meaning requires.
- **Motif:** reuse the **window-grid / stacked-units** idea and simplified building forms.
- **People:** simple, inclusive, warm characters; avoid overly cartoonish or quirky styles.
- **Consistency:** one illustration system (same line weight, corner rounding, color logic) across product and marketing.

## Abstract visual direction
For backgrounds, hero accents, dividers, and decorative moments:

- **Window-grid pattern** — the signature motif; tileable square grid derived from the façade. Use at low contrast as a background texture, or with a few "lit" gold squares as an accent.
- **Architectural line work** — subtle blueprint-like lines, isometric building hints.
- **Navy gradients** (`#16293B → #0D1B28`) with sparing gold glow for premium depth.
- Keep abstract elements **quiet and supportive** — never competing with content or the gold focal point.

## Building / architecture imagery
- Favor **residential** buildings that match the product (apartment blocks, mixed mid-rise), not corporate skyscrapers or luxury villas.
- Show **lit windows, entrances, and shared spaces** — the parts the product is about.
- Architectural, respectful framing; clean skies or calm backgrounds.
- Can be paired with the window-grid motif and navy/gold grade for a cohesive brand feel.

## Community imagery
- Center **people living together well**: neighbours, building committees, families, residents using a phone to check the building.
- Warm, candid, inclusive, and regionally authentic (Arabic-speaking contexts represented naturally).
- Convey **trust, fairness, belonging** — the emotional needs from `01-strategy/04_TARGET_AUDIENCE.md`.
- Avoid conflict/stress imagery; show the calm, well-run outcome.

## What imagery to avoid
- ❌ Generic, obviously-staged corporate stock (handshakes in suits, fake smiles).
- ❌ Cold, empty, luxury-skyscraper or glossy real-estate-sales imagery.
- ❌ Harsh, oversaturated, or heavily filtered photos that clash with the navy/gold grade.
- ❌ Cluttered, busy compositions with no space for text.
- ❌ Cliché finance imagery (piggy banks, raining money, coins stacks as the hero).
- ❌ Off-brand color casts (e.g. heavy greens/purples) that fight the palette.
- ❌ Non-inclusive or culturally mismatched casting.
- ❌ Low-resolution or distorted images; never stretch to fit.

## Social media image rules
- **Formats:** Square 1080×1080, Portrait 1080×1350, Story/Reel 1080×1920, plus platform cover sizes (specifics in `05-marketing/01_SOCIAL_MEDIA_GUIDE.md`).
- **Safe zones:** keep text/logo clear of platform UI overlays (caption bars, profile icons on stories).
- **Branding:** include the logo in clear space (correct variant for the background); use a single gold accent.
- **Type:** follow the marketing type scale; short, high-contrast headlines; bilingual versions (RTL for Arabic).
- **Grade:** apply the navy/gold treatment for a recognizable, cohesive feed.
- **Contrast:** navy scrim behind text over photos; meet contrast minimums.
- **Consistency:** templatize so posts share grid, margins, and style.
- **Accessibility:** add descriptive **alt text**; don't bury essential info in the image only.

## Sourcing, licensing & alt text
- Use properly **licensed** or original photography/illustration; record the license/source.
- Prefer original/commissioned assets for hero moments to avoid generic stock.
- Provide **alt text** for all meaningful images (concise, descriptive, localized).
- Export web imagery in modern formats (WebP/AVIF) at appropriate resolutions; never upscale.
