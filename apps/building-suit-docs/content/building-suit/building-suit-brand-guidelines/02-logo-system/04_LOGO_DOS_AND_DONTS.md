# Logo Do's and Don'ts

> **Status:** Complete (initial). Approved and prohibited uses of the Building Suit logo, shown by example. Read with `02_LOGO_USAGE_RULES.md` and `03_LOGO_CLEAR_SPACE.md`. The logo is approved — these rules protect it; they do not change it.

---

## Correct usage examples
✅ **Do:**
- Use the supplied master files unchanged, at their original proportions.
- Choose the variant with the strongest contrast (dark/navy on light; light/silver on dark).
- Keep the full mark intact — building, windows, door, and lettered bowls.
- Give the logo its full clear space (`1X`, see clear-space doc).
- Keep the gold windows their approved gold.
- Keep the 3D rendering and lighting exactly as supplied.
- Place the logo upright on a calm, high-contrast background.

## Incorrect usage examples
❌ **Don't:**
- Stretch, squash, or distort the logo.
- Recolor the body or the gold windows/door.
- Rotate, flip, or mirror the artwork.
- Re-render, simplify, or flatten the 3D mark by hand.
- Add shadows, glows, outlines, gradients, or other effects.
- Place on low-contrast or busy backgrounds.
- Crop the mark or violate its clear space.
- Use a low-resolution, screenshotted, or recompressed copy.

## Rules about not stretching
- **Always lock the aspect ratio.** Scale proportionally (drive by height, given the tall mark).
- Never stretch horizontally or vertically, never skew, never apply non-uniform scale.
- Never CSS-stretch in web/app layouts — serve a correctly sized asset instead.
- Do not upscale beyond the raster master's native resolution (920 × 1245 px); request a vector master for large sizes.

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Proportional scale (height-locked) | Horizontal/vertical stretch |
| Serve correct asset size | CSS width/height distortion |
| Vector for large print | Upscaled blurry raster |

## Rules about not recoloring gold bars
The gold windows and door are the brand's premium accent and a core equity — they are **fixed**.

- **Never** recolor the gold windows/door to another hue (no silver, blue, green, red windows).
- **Never** turn the windows off (don't remove or blank them) — the "lit" look is essential.
- **Never** change the body colour either (navy stays navy; silver stays silver).
- Don't apply gradients, tints, or overlays that shift the gold.
- The only approved colour states are the two supplied variants (navy-body and silver-body), each with the **same approved gold**.

## Rules about not changing the 3D effect
The 3D form, bevels, and lighting are part of the approved identity.

- **Don't** flatten the logo to a 2D shape by hand, or rebuild it as an outline.
- **Don't** alter the extrusion depth, perspective, or the direction/intensity of the lighting.
- **Don't** add or remove highlights, reflections, or material texture.
- If a **flat/single-colour** mark is needed (tiny sizes, one-colour print), use the dedicated flat companion mark once produced — **do not** improvise one from the 3D logo. (That companion asset is currently outstanding; see source notes.)

## Rules about not adding random shadows
- The logo already includes its own soft contact shadow/lighting — **don't add extra** drop shadows, outer glows, or strokes.
- **Don't** place it on a hand-made shadow box, badge, or container "to make it pop."
- If the background is busy, fix the *background* (use a calm area or a subtle, even scrim) — don't bolt effects onto the logo.
- No bevels, embossing, reflections, or "shine" added on top of the supplied artwork.

## Rules about background contrast
Contrast is the deciding factor for which variant to use.

| Background | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Light (white, light grey, pale) | **Dark / navy** logo | Light/silver logo (washes out) |
| Dark (navy, charcoal, deep photo) | **Light / silver** logo | Dark/navy logo (disappears) |
| Mid-tone (e.g. medium grey/blue) | Whichever gives clear contrast; add a scrim if borderline | Either logo where it merges into the tone |
| Gold | **Dark / navy** logo, sparingly | Light/silver logo on gold (low contrast) |
| Busy imagery | Calm area or subtle scrim, then correct variant | Logo dropped onto detailed/cluttered areas |

- Aim for strong, comfortable contrast — when in doubt, increase it.
- Never rely on the gold windows alone for contrast against the background.

## Designer checklist
Before shipping any logo placement, confirm:

- [ ] Used an **approved master file** (not a screenshot/recompressed copy).
- [ ] Correct **variant for the background** (dark on light / light on dark; navy-only on gold).
- [ ] **Aspect ratio locked** — no stretching, skewing, or distortion.
- [ ] **Gold windows/door unchanged**; body colour unchanged.
- [ ] **3D effect and lighting unchanged**; not flattened or rebuilt.
- [ ] **No added** shadows, glows, outlines, gradients, or containers.
- [ ] **Upright** — not rotated, flipped, or mirrored (mirror placement only in RTL, never the artwork).
- [ ] **Clear space** (`1X`) respected; safe area free of other elements.
- [ ] **Above minimum size** (40 px / 12 mm); used app-icon mark for smaller contexts.
- [ ] **Sufficient contrast** with the background (scrim added if borderline).
- [ ] Correct **resolution** for the medium; vector requested for large print.
