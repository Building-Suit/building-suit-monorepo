## Page 04 — Brand Story

**Page title**

Why Building Suit exists

**Polished copy**

Shared buildings often depend on group chats, notebooks, cash envelopes, and one person who remembers everything. Residents cannot reliably see where money goes, decisions disappear in conversation, and administration is difficult to transfer.

Building Suit creates one auditable record for the building—so responsibility can be shared, history can be preserved, and trust can grow.

**Key points**

- **Fragmented:** information lives everywhere.
- **Uncertain:** money and decisions are difficult to verify.
- **Structured:** every item has a place, state, and owner.

## Page 09 — Logo Clear Space

**Page title**

Give the mark room to lead

**Polished copy**

Clear space protects recognition and keeps the logo feeling premium. Nothing should compete with the mark inside its protected area.

- Mirror placement for RTL; never mirror the artwork.

## Page 19 — Iconography

**Page title**

One visual language for every action

**Polished copy**

Building Suit icons are flat, line-first, calm, and consistent. Their geometry supports the interface without competing with content.

**Key points**

- 24 × 24 grid; approximately 20 px live area.
- 2 px default stroke with rounded caps and joins.
- Default = neutral outline.
- Active = Stroke Rounded + Premium Gold + label/indicator.
- Semantic icons use status colors, not Gold.
- Mirror directional icons in RTL only.
- Use only the free [Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com/) Stroke Rounded library. Solid, Duotone, Twotone, Bulk, and other paid styles are not permitted until licensed.
- Do not mix Hugeicons with Lucide, Phosphor, or other icon families. Create custom icons only when Hugeicons has no clear equivalent, matching its visual construction.
