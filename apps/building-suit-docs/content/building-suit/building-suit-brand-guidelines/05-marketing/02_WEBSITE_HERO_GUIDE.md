# Website Hero Guide

> **Status:** Complete (initial). Brand direction for the marketing website hero. Source of truth: `01-strategy/` (messaging/voice), `02-logo-system/`, `03-visual-identity/01_COLOR_SYSTEM.md`, `02_TYPOGRAPHY_SYSTEM.md`, `04_LAYOUT_AND_SPACING.md`. The hero is the brand's biggest first impression — premium, clear, one message, one action.

---

## Website hero layout
- **Structure (desktop):** two-column — left = headline + subheadline + CTA(s); right = app mockup / brand visual. Or centered single-column for a bolder statement.
- **Grid:** 12 columns, content max 1200–1280px, generous margins; large top/bottom spacing (64–96px).
- **One primary CTA** (plus optional secondary/ghost). One focal point.
- **Logo** in a slim sticky header (top-left LTR / top-right RTL).
- **Responsive:** stacks to single column on mobile (headline → CTA → mockup); type scales down via the web→mobile ramp.
- **RTL:** fully mirrored for Arabic.

## Headline style
- **Font:** Manrope ExtraBold (800); web Hero scale ~56/64 (clamp down on mobile).
- **Copy:** benefit-led, short, confident — e.g. **"See exactly where your building's money goes."** or **"Run your building transparently."**
- **Color:** Pearl White on navy hero / Graphite on light hero; optionally **one gold accent word**.
- Keep to 1–2 lines; protect negative space.

## Subheadline style
- **Font:** Manrope Regular/Medium; Body L ~18–20.
- **Copy:** one supporting sentence on what it is / who it's for — e.g. "One transparent app for charges, payments, issues, announcements, and decisions."
- **Color:** slightly muted (Sky Steel on navy / Slate Gray on light).
- One or two lines max.

---

## CTA style
- **Primary CTA:** on navy hero → **Premium Gold button**, Deep Navy text; on light hero → **Building Navy button**, Pearl White text. Radius 12px, SemiBold label (e.g. "Get started", "Download the app").
- **Secondary CTA:** outline/ghost ("See how it works") beside the primary.
- One primary CTA only; clear, action-led labels.
- Store badges (App Store / Google Play) may sit below the CTA for the app.

## Logo usage
- **Header logo:** dark/navy logo on a light header; **light/silver logo on a navy header**; ~32–48px tall with clear space.
- The hero **brand visual** may feature the 3D building-B larger as a premium moment (on a calm area, strong contrast).
- Favicon = app-icon mark (see `02-logo-system/05_APP_ICON_GUIDE.md`).
- Never stretch/recolor/add effects to the logo.

---

## Background style
- **Default (recommended):** Building Navy `#16293B` → Deep Structure Navy `#0D1B28` gradient, with subtle **window-grid motif** and a faint gold glow — premium and on-brand.
- **Light alternative:** Pearl White with a navy/gold accent shape or product visual.
- Keep backgrounds quiet; never compete with the headline or the single gold focal point.
- Optional architectural/community photo with a navy scrim for text contrast.

## App mockup placement
- Show the **product on a phone** (mobile-first) — a clean device frame angled or straight, on the hero's right (LTR) / left (RTL).
- Display a real, on-brand screen (e.g. dashboard with balance, or a payment/vote card).
- Add soft depth (subtle shadow/float); keep within clear space; don't crop key UI.
- Multiple devices optional, but keep it uncluttered; one hero phone is often strongest.
- Ensure the mockup screen follows the UI system (light or dark mode consistent with the hero).

- **Logo:** light/silver.
- **Headline:** Pearl White (+ optional gold accent word).
- **Subheadline:** Sky Steel.
- **Primary CTA:** Premium Gold button, Deep Navy text.
- **Mockup:** dark-mode app screen for cohesion (or light screen for contrast pop).

- **Subheadline:** Slate Gray.

## Supporting sections (below the hero)
- Maintain rhythm: alternating navy/light sections, 48–96px spacing, consistent grid.
- Feature blocks: icon (brand icon style) + short title + one line; one benefit each.
- Keep one gold accent per section; let whitespace carry the premium feel.
- Bilingual parity (English + Arabic RTL) throughout.
