# Business Card Guide

> **Status:** Complete (initial). Specifications for Building Suit business cards. Source of truth: `02-logo-system/`, `03-visual-identity/01_COLOR_SYSTEM.md` + `02_TYPOGRAPHY_SYSTEM.md`. Premium, minimal, navy-and-gold — the brand in the hand.

---

## Specifications
| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Size (standard) | 85 × 55 mm (or 90 × 50 mm / US 3.5 × 2 in) |
| Bleed | +3 mm all sides |
| Safe area | 4–5 mm inside trim (keep text/logo inside) |
| Corners | square (or 3mm rounded to echo the soft brand geometry) |
| Orientation | landscape (default) |
| Color mode | CMYK for print; supply spot/Pantone for gold + navy if budget allows |
| Resolution | 300 dpi minimum |

## Front side
- **Purpose:** brand impression — logo-forward, minimal.
- **Background:** Building Navy `#16293B` (premium default).
- **Logo:** **light/silver logo** centered or left-aligned, with full clear space; gold windows read beautifully on navy.
- Optional: small tagline "Clarity you can trust." in Pearl White below the logo.
- Keep it clean — no contact details on the front.

## Back side
- **Purpose:** contact details.
- **Background:** Pearl White `#F7F8FA` (or White), or navy with light text — keep front/back contrast intentional.
- **Logo:** small **dark/navy logo** (top corner) if the back is light.
- **Content:** name, role, contact details; one gold accent (a rule, the role, or an icon set).
- Generous margins; left-aligned (LTR) / right-aligned (RTL).

## Logo placement
- Front: light/silver logo on navy, with ≥1X clear space, optically centered/left.
- Back: small dark/navy logo on light (corner).
- Never stretch, recolor the gold, rotate, or add effects (see `02-logo-system/04_LOGO_DOS_AND_DONTS.md`).
- Request a **vector master** for crisp print (raster PNG is not ideal at card scale — see source notes).

## Typography
| Element | Font | Size (approx) | Weight | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Manrope | 10–11 pt | Bold (700) | Graphite / Pearl White |
| Role / title | Manrope | 8–9 pt | Medium (500) | Slate Gray / Sky Steel |
| Contact details | Manrope | 7–8 pt | Regular/Medium | Graphite / Pearl White |
| Tagline | Manrope | 8–9 pt | SemiBold | Pearl White (on navy) |
| Arabic (if bilingual) | IBM Plex Sans Arabic | match sizes | Medium/Bold | as above |

- Keep to 2–3 weights; clear hierarchy; don't crowd.

---

## Colors
- **Navy** `#16293B` (front), **Pearl White** `#F7F8FA` (back), **Premium Gold** `#D89B42` as a single accent.
- Gold accent ideas: a thin rule, the icon bullets, or the role line — used once, sparingly.
- Optional premium finish: **gold foil** for the logo windows/accent, spot UV, or soft-touch lamination (see print notes).

## Contact information layout
Recommended back-side order (left-aligned LTR):
```
[small navy logo]            (top corner)

Full Name            — Name (Bold)
Role / Title         — muted

m  +20 ...           — mobile
e  name@buildingsuit… — email
w  buildingsuit.…     — website
   @handle            — social (optional)
```
- Use small brand icons (phone, email, web) in muted color with one gold accent.
- Align labels/values on a tidy grid; keep within the safe area.
- **Bilingual:** Arabic on the back (or a dedicated Arabic side) — RTL, right-aligned, mirrored layout; keep "Building Suit" in Latin.

## Print notes
- **Bleed 3 mm**, **safe area 4–5 mm**; keep all text/logo inside safe area.
- **CMYK**; pin navy and gold targets — supply **Pantone/spot** for consistent navy + gold if possible.
- **Gold:** flat CMYK gold can look dull — prefer **metallic Pantone or gold foil** for the premium effect; confirm with printer.
- **Stock:** 350–400 gsm; matte/soft-touch lamination reads premium; optional spot UV or foil on the logo.
- Supply **vector** artwork (PDF/X) with outlined fonts and embedded profiles.
- Proof a physical sample before a full run (especially for gold/foil).
