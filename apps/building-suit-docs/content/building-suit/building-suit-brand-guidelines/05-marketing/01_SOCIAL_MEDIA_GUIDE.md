## Instagram post style
- **Mood:** premium, clean, visual-first; navy or photo background with one gold accent.
- **Layout:** strong central or lower-third headline; generous negative space; logo in a corner with clear space.
- **Type:** Manrope ExtraBold/Bold headline (marketing scale), short and benefit-led; IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic posts (RTL, right-aligned).
- **Color:** Building Navy `#16293B` base, Pearl White text, **one Premium Gold accent word or element**.
- **Imagery:** lit buildings, community, or window-grid motif with the navy/gold grade (see `03-visual-identity/06_IMAGERY_STYLE.md`).
- **Carousels:** consistent template across slides; cover hooks, last slide = CTA.

## LinkedIn post style
- **Mood:** more professional and credible (audience: founders, partners, B2B), still premium and warm.
- **Layout:** clear headline + supporting line; can be more text-forward; data/quote cards work well.
- **Type:** Manrope; concise, confident, no hype.
- **Color:** navy/white base, gold accent for the key figure or word; semantic colors only for real data.
- **Content:** product value, transparency stories, milestones, hiring, thought leadership.

## Story style
- **Vertical 1080 × 1920**; keep text/logo in the **central safe zone** (clear of top/bottom platform UI).
- One idea per story; large type; a single tap-forward CTA or swipe-up/link sticker.
- Navy background or photo with navy scrim; gold accent for emphasis.
- Use subtle motion (fade/slide) if animated; keep it calm.
- Series/templates for announcements, tips, and behind-the-scenes.

## Launch announcement style
- **Hero treatment:** Building Navy → Deep Structure Navy gradient, the **light/silver logo or 3D building-B** as a brand moment, gold accent.
- **Headline:** the launch message in Manrope ExtraBold (e.g. "Building Suit is here — clarity you can trust.").
- **Supporting:** one line of value + clear CTA (download / learn more).
- **Set:** matching post + story + LinkedIn variants; bilingual (English + Arabic RTL) versions.
- Keep it premium and uncluttered — let the navy/gold and the mark carry it.

## Logo placement
- Use the **app-icon mark** for avatars; the **full logo** for covers and in-post branding.
- **Variant by background:** dark/navy logo on light; **light/silver logo on dark/navy** (the usual social case).
- Always keep **clear space** (≥1X) around the logo; place in a corner, off platform-UI overlays.
- Never stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects (see `02-logo-system/04_LOGO_DOS_AND_DONTS.md`).

## Color usage
- **Base:** Building Navy `#16293B` (or Pearl White for light posts).
- **Text:** Pearl White on navy / Graphite on light.
- **Accent:** Premium Gold `#D89B42` — one focal element per asset (word, underline, icon, shape).
- **Gradients:** navy gradient for depth; gold gradient only for special hero moments.
- **Semantic colors** only for genuine data/status, never decoration.
- Keep a recognizable, consistent navy/gold feed — don't drift into off-brand colors.

---

## Text overlay rules
- **Contrast first:** navy scrim/gradient behind text over photos; meet AA contrast.
- Short, punchy, benefit-led copy (brand voice); one headline + optional one supporting line.
- Don't fill the frame with text — protect negative space.
- Arabic overlays: right-aligned, RTL, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, no caps/tracking; keep product name "Building Suit" in Latin.
- Keep essential info out of the image only — repeat key points in the caption + **alt text**.

---

## Captions, hashtags & handles
- Voice: clear, warm, confident; no hype. Lead with the benefit.
- Provide **bilingual** captions where the audience is mixed (English + Arabic).
- Use a small, consistent hashtag set (brand + category + local); avoid hashtag walls.
- Keep handle/name consistent across platforms.

## Do / Don't examples
| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Navy base + one gold accent | Rainbow of colors / multiple golds |
| App-icon mark for avatars | Full tall logo squeezed into avatar |
| Light logo on navy, dark logo on light | Logo that blends into the background |
| Short, benefit-led headline | Paragraphs of text on the image |
| Navy scrim behind text on photos | Low-contrast text over busy photos |
| Bilingual + alt text | Essential info only inside the image |
| Consistent templates across a series | A different look every post |
| Premium, calm composition | Cluttered, salesy, hype-heavy posts |
