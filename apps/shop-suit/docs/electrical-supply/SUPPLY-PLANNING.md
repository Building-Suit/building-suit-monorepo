Bins and shelf positions describe where existing stock is kept. They do not introduce independent quantities or valuation. Product positions can reference physical reels; a reel placement uses the existing authorized reel command and its immutable movement history without changing physical length or FIFO value. Location, product and bin ownership must match. Archive commands retain history and require active placements to be cleared first.

Supplier references are location/product/vendor scoped, with supplier item code, approved purchase unit or versioned package, lead time and review days. A preferred supplier is explicit. Existing issued documents never change when references change. Price history reads original ordinary purchase lines and their currency/unit snapshots, including clearly labelled void documents. It never converts currencies or silently compares unlike stock units.

Reorder suggestions extend the existing operational report route. They explain the selected historical demand window, net canonical issued quantity after returns/corrections, free stock, open supplier-order quantity, configured lead/review days and exact rounded-to-increment suggestion. Physical sources are counted through existing FIFO batches; holds are subtracted once. Suggestions write no stock, orders, prices or payments. Missing preferred supplier/history is explicit; zero demand is not invented demand. Merchant review remains necessary.


An active structured reel placement is the authority for its existing POS/reel position label. The planning command changes its metadata and invokes the existing authorized reel command atomically. A private trigger rejects conflicting free-text position edits through the older reel screen, which directs staff to Storage & supply planning. Unassigned reels retain their original free-text path. Clear physical placements before changing a bin code/name; clear all active placements before archival. All source lengths, costs, classification/return/hold guards and immutable movement history remain authoritative.

The report reads held quantities, ordinary supplier receipts, on-hand batches and open promises in one statement snapshot, instead of repeatedly invoking the intentionally volatile mutation helpers. Its demand window uses Cairo business dates: issued product lines in the selected period, excluding corrected documents, less returns on those lines through the period end. Missing supplier access hides private reference names/codes/timing and offers no suggestion. Missing/archived/revision-stale preferred references also offer no suggestion. Otherwise the greater of configured reorder threshold and net daily demand times lead/review days is reduced by free stock and open incoming quantities, then rounded up to the product increment or the preferred whole package. Every displayed quantity remains canonical and is never summed across physical units.
