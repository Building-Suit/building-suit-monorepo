# Customer groups and trade prices

This E4 feature extends existing clients, catalog products and invoice/payment history. It introduces no financial ledger, operational product registration, subscription price or entitlement change.

Groups are tenant-scoped and configurable. Assigning or archiving a group changes future price eligibility and preserves balances and issued documents. Price definitions and assignments use revision checks, caller-bound request IDs, actor/reason history and the existing actor/subscription write checks. Configuration does not post money or lock an unused shop’s currency; invoice posting retains the existing period/currency guards. Product managers maintain prices and approve overrides; existing client managers maintain groups. Reading negotiated definitions requires client visibility and product-management authority; authorized cashiers receive applicable prices through the sale quote.

Pricing precedence is deterministic: an explicitly selected remnant retains its source price, followed by an effective customer contract, effective customer group, quantity break, fixed package and base catalog price. An authorized explicit override supersedes the resolved price. Within a scope, higher priority wins, then the largest applicable canonical quantity break, newest creation time and stable ID. Effective time is inclusive; expiry is exclusive. Thresholds aggregate canonical quantities of the same product across cart lines, including different compatible selling units. Package rules require the exact package/version. Physical units use the sealed conversion registry; legacy products retain their ordinary quantity contract.

Rules freeze the product's stock unit, measurement revision and shop currency. A currency change in an unused shop cannot reinterpret old negotiated amounts in the new currency. A later unused-product reconfiguration cannot reinterpret an old length price as a mass price. Stale rules stop matching until a manager reviews their definition. Archiving retains the old definition and remains possible after product/package retirement or reconfiguration.

Trade prices and bundle discounts never stack: a cart using any trade price or approved override receives no bundle discount. Ordinary carts retain their existing bundle matching. An explicit ordinary-sales order discount remains available and is preserved at issuance; the automatic-offer removal does not erase it. Drafts reprice at issue, retaining the exact chosen rule revision. Existing POS and fast-pay commands check the collected amount again after issue; a changed total rolls back issuance, FIFO and collection for review rather than recording a partial payment. Issued line and receipt snapshots remain immutable. Customer/quantity/unit/source changes invalidate a retained staff approval. A cashier can quote, save notes on and issue the same approved draft without acquiring product-management authority; a new or changed override requires a manager. The original approving actor/reason and final entered price remain server-owned history.

POS and ordinary sales send customer and existing-draft context to the server quote. Query identity includes actor, shop, location, customer, draft and line inputs. Late responses cannot overwrite changed-scope configuration. Displayed cart totals come from the quote. Local previews never authorize prices or stock. Offline checkout requires a confirmed disabled tax/trade-pricing configuration; active or unknown trade pricing requires connection. Manual cart entry remains available, and ordinary stores with confirmed disabled policies retain their existing offline path.

Group/rule/history views use a shared complete template, typed projections and bounded server pages. All entity choices load through paged, scoped adapters. A lost write response freezes the request and its values for an identical retry. No save is announced as successful without a confirmed response. English/Arabic and both directions/themes use existing shared controls.

The forward migration extends the actual reviewed sale authority, preserving grants and using body guards for the replaced private routines. Issuance retains sorted product locks and holds the client/group rows while resolving prices. Physical reel planning, FIFO acquisition cost, period checks, tax and payment ledgers keep their existing authorities. The generated database contract comes from the replayed schema.

Qualification evidence is maintained in VERIFICATION.md and evidence.json. Formal quotation revisions, sales orders, stock reservations, deposits/partial fulfillment and supplier ordering/deliveries remain separate E4 work.
