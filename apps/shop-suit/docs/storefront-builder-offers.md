# Customer storefront builder and catalogue bundles

The customer website follows the supplied Belanjain.Aja shopping layout using Building Suit tokens: a compact merchant header, an optional uploaded banner, overlapping product/service search, offer strip, catalogue, gallery, team, branches and inquiry/appointment access. Without a banner the search starts at the top. No grocery-specific content, stock claims, countdowns, payment-provider badges or fictitious staff are seeded.

`/website-builder` requires `settings.manage`; the database resolves the owner's effective, writable Team or Multi entitlement on every read/save and public projection. Other plans receive an upgrade gate. Downgrades retain the saved layout but omit its custom media/team/offers from the public response. Draft edits never change the published layout. Hidden gallery/team sections are omitted from the anonymous payload, not only from the rendered page. Only the owner publishes the saved draft, after the public shop profile is published. Optimistic revisions reject concurrent edits. Public staff are independently entered names, roles, portraits and introductions; private employee records are never copied. Gallery (8), staff (20) and raster byte/dimension limits are checked at the command boundary. Shared UX re-encodes selected raster uploads before submission.

`/offers` uses authorized, tenant-scoped catalogue queries and the canonical table/dialog controllers. A bundle contains 1–20 active products/services with positive three-decimal quantities, a fixed positive price below its current regular net price, activation, optional time range and explicit matching priority. Lower priorities apply first, followed by creation time and ID. Complete sets repeat; a quantity cannot be consumed twice. Remaining items keep their regular price. An offer whose current regular price falls below its configured price is skipped. Public offers additionally require Team/Multi, explicit publication and every item individually published with its price visible; public baskets support at most 10 distinct items.

POS selection adds the complete recipe atomically after checking location stock. Selecting the same items normally invokes the same server matching. `quote_pos_sale` and the existing idempotent POS draft path use one private matcher. Discounts feed the existing order-discount tax allocator; FIFO, cash-shift policy, appointment linkage, payment and returns remain in their established commands. Offer name, revision, bundle count, price and savings freeze into issued/paid receipt snapshots. Reprints and shared receipts use those saved values after an offer changes. Offline estimates use cached recipes; replay recalculates and rejects changed totals through the existing reconciliation flow rather than silently changing a collected amount.

Public basket submissions remain merchant-confirmation inquiries, not paid orders or inventory reservations. The server maps public catalogue identifiers to active tenant items, rechecks publication, matches only publicly eligible offers and appends the current bundle names/prices to the private owner inbox. Client totals are indicative. Existing booking/customer access routes, host isolation and Origin checks are retained.
