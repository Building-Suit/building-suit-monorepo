# Appointment email reminders

Delivery is **disabled by default**. This task provides the complete worker and a dormant supported scheduler, and installs no enabled job, provider configuration, keys, DNS changes, hosted migration or customer enrollment. It does not send emails. `SHOP_REMINDER_DELIVERY_MODE=test` enforces an exact recipient allowlist; explicit `live` mode supports all currently eligible consented recipients under the same database budgets. General delivery requires approved configuration, not another feature build.

## Consent and eligibility

The public booking readiness flag reflects this owner policy; it never promises provider delivery or exposes operator secrets. An active Shop owner with `settings.manage` and write access explicitly enables email reminders in the existing booking policy. That does not enroll customers. A customer uses their existing short-lived, phone-verified customer access session to request one reminder for one upcoming booked appointment. Their active saved customer record must contain a syntactically valid email. Email addresses are not returned to the browser or public website. A phone verification proves the customer scope; it does **not** independently verify the email inbox. Operators must maintain accurate contact data; this feature never guesses or collects an arbitrary destination. Guests and appointments without an eligible saved customer email remain ineligible.

Consent records bind the appointment, tenant, customer, exact normalized email, locale and authorizing credential. A contact change/deactivation or customer reassignment withdraws prior consent. Rescheduling retains consent for the same customer/contact and queues the new future appointment time; the previous intent is cancelled. A changed appointment instant never reuses its old provider key. Repeated consent cannot create duplicate queue rows. A reminder that may already have been attempted can never be resurrected. A cancelled intent with zero provider attempts and no frozen payload can be requested again before its due time. A customer may opt out from appointment management; owners may disable the policy. Staff can cancel the appointment or correct the customer contact through their existing authorized commands.

The queue checks current Shop, published website, booking policy, location, appointment status/time, customer identity, contact, consent and delivery mode (including the allowlist in test mode) when claiming and again under locks during prepare. Unpublishing/disabling/suspending prevents subsequent authorization. No automatic enrollment or backfill occurs; consent after the requested reminder time does not produce a catch-up email. The preferred lead time is elapsed minutes before the UTC appointment instant. The email formats that instant in the staff schedule's IANA timezone, with the timezone displayed; no server/browser-local timezone is used. Missing schedule timezone falls back explicitly to UTC.

## Delivery, races and budgets

Private outbox/consent/audit/budget tables have RLS and no direct grants to browser or service roles. Browser RPCs require the existing current tenant/customer capability. Owner policy writes retain owner, permission, subscription checks and private audit events. Worker RPCs are service-role only. Old wrapped functions are revoked from browser roles.

Workers claim one committed row using tenant-first locks, `SKIP LOCKED`, a two-minute lease and random fence. `prepare_appointment_email` rechecks authorization after lock waits, freezes the exact provider payload and consumes the allowance before HTTP. A claim is not permission to send. After waiting for the final global budget lock, prepare rechecks lease expiry, appointment start and the 23-hour deadline. A final conditional authorization rechecks those clocks and rolls back any allowance writes if a deadline passes; waiting cannot extend a grant. Tests race cancellation, reschedule and contact changes between claim and prepare. The prepare commit is the authorization point: a cancellation after it cannot recall an already in-flight HTTP request. It cancels all retries; a matching late provider acceptance receipt is retained without reviving the queue.

Provider POSTs use `shop-appointment/<outbox UUID>` as a stable Resend idempotency key. Unknown HTTP outcomes and retryable 409/429/5xx responses reuse the frozen payload and key. Other rejection statuses stop for review. There are at most five provider attempts, exponential backoff (2, 4, 8, 16, 32 minutes), no retry at/after 23 hours from first attempt, and no attempt after appointment start. These bounds stay inside Resend's documented 24-hour idempotency window. A successful response means **accepted**, not delivered. No bounce automatically resends. Provider response bodies, credentials and message content are never logged or returned.

SQL serializes shared reminder budgets across workers/tenants: 80 provider attempts per UTC day, 2,400 per UTC month, 10 per tenant per UTC day and at most one authorization per second. Retries count too. There is no paid fallback, purchase or automatic quota increase. These are reminder allocations below the documented Resend Free limits (100/day, 3,000/month), **not a claim that other Auth/support mail has been counted**. Before activation the operator must review total shared-account usage and reserve adequate headroom for Auth/support. Provider throttles defer bounded retries; exhausted allowances keep the item deferred until eligible/expired. The queue requires operational monitoring of age, review-required states and provider receipts before broader activation.

## Activation configuration (names and destinations only)

No values are supplied or changed by this task.

| Key | Destination / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `SHOP_REMINDER_DELIVERY_MODE` | Shop Supabase Edge secret; absent/`disabled` refuses sending; `test` requires the allowlist; `live` requires explicit operator approval before configuration |
| `SHOP_REMINDER_TEST_ALLOWLIST` | Same Edge environment; comma-separated exact approved test email addresses, maximum 20, no wildcard; enforced in test mode only |
| `SHOP_REMINDER_WORKER_TOKEN` | Same Edge environment and approved scheduler's secret store; dedicated random token, minimum 32 characters |
| `SHOP_REMINDER_FROM` | Same Edge environment; approved verified `@building-suit.com` sender mailbox; no guessed mailbox |
| `RESEND_API_KEY` | Same Edge environment; existing Resend integration, authorized sender scope; never public Nuxt configuration |
| `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` | Shop project Edge-provided server environment; separate staging/production projects |
| `shop_reminder_project_url`, `shop_reminder_server_key`, `shop_reminder_worker_token` | That same project's Vault, provisioned by the approved configuration operator only |
| `SHOP_REMINDER_SCHEDULE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` | Approved environment-bound CI secret for the operator RPC; never public/runtime client configuration |
| `SHOP_REMINDER_SCHEDULE_APPROVED`, `SHOP_REMINDER_APPROVED_SHA` | Approved CI job inputs/environment, exact review decision and source SHA binding; not secret values |

`POST <verified Shop project URL>/functions/v1/shop-appointment-reminder-sender` requires the platform-default JWT verification plus `Authorization: Bearer <Shop service-role JWT>` and `X-Shop-Reminder-Worker-Token: <dedicated token>`. No `verify_jwt=false` configuration is added. This endpoint is server-only and has no browser CORS flow. One invocation handles at most one reminder, with 10-second provider and 15-second RPC timeouts.

## Supported dormant scheduling and exact activation

The forward migration implements the existing Shop `pg_cron`/`pg_net`/Vault pattern used by XPay. It creates disabled schedule settings and narrowly granted operator RPC `shop_appointment_email_schedule(boolean,text)`. **It does not register or activate a cron job.** The fixed task-owned job runs once per minute and calls `shop_private.appointment_email_scheduled_request()`. The callback refuses disabled settings, validates all bindings, loads secrets at execution time, and POSTs only `{}` to the exact owned Shop project endpoint with both credentials. The cron command stores no secrets. Missing/duplicate/mismatched bindings fail closed. Enable calls upsert the same named job; disable unschedules only this task-owned job and remains available when secrets are missing. Configuration changes are privately audited.

Activation after approval requires configuration only:

1. Parent integrates the final reviewed source through the normal protected GitHub path. The existing Supabase GitHub integration deploys the versioned Shop migrations and Edge function for the selected environment; use its configured `stg`/`main` branch and `apps/shop-suit` working directory. Do not run a parallel/manual migration or function deployer. Verify provider success, the exact source SHA, default JWT verification and extension availability before configuration. This task does not authorize production promotion.
2. The authorized configuration operator supplies the Edge keys listed above for that same project. Start with `test` and approved synthetic/test-recipient addresses. Leave the mode absent/disabled until delivery configuration is approved. Review total shared Resend usage, including Auth/support and both environments, before allocating the conservative reminder budgets; never enable a paid fallback or overages as part of activation.
3. The approved configuration pipeline supplies **Vault** entries `shop_reminder_project_url` (exact `https://<projectRef>.supabase.co`), `shop_reminder_server_key` (that project's service-role JWT), and `shop_reminder_worker_token` (exactly the dedicated Edge token). These entries are not created by migrations or tests. Staging is `jvvelvftpfnlogalgxgv`; production is `fgdzjnsbcxfbiuogbmom`. The scheduler accepts only these immutable registry bindings and rejects arbitrary endpoints or a URL/ref mismatch.
4. In the existing approved environment-bound **CI** configuration job, invoke `node tooling/database/configure-shop-reminder-schedule.mjs staging enable` (or the separately approved `production enable`). Supply `SHOP_REMINDER_SCHEDULE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` as a CI secret, `SHOP_REMINDER_SCHEDULE_APPROVED=true`, and `SHOP_REMINDER_APPROVED_SHA` equal to that job's `GITHUB_SHA`. The helper requires real CI/GitHub Actions context and `refs/heads/stg` or `refs/heads/main` respectively, derives the endpoint from the maintained environment registry, refuses redirects, and calls the operator RPC. It returns only enabled/project/job metadata. It never reads or writes Vault secrets and never sends email directly. If transport/result validation fails, inspect the private settings/events and fixed named job before assuming activation did not commit; repeating the operation upserts that same job. No workflow enabling it is installed by this task.
5. Confirm the fixed named job, private schedule events, pg_net request outcomes and the worker's test-recipient results. A minute cadence is at most 44,640 invocations in a 31-day month, below the documented Free Edge allowance before other project usage. The global/day/month/tenant/rate limits, consent and current appointment checks apply equally to test and live requests.
6. After explicit customer-delivery approval, configure `SHOP_REMINDER_DELIVERY_MODE=live` through that same approved environment configuration process. No source change is needed. Test allowlists remain intact for returning to test mode. Do not activate live mode or a job from a feature branch, local machine, personal network, n8n or another service.
7. To stop future scheduled invocations, use the same approved CI helper with `staging disable`/`production disable`; it invokes `p_enabled=false` and requires no functioning Vault binding. Remove/disable the Edge delivery mode as well. In-flight requests retain the documented authorization-point limitation; no terminal receipt/configuration is deleted.

No activation, secret creation/configuration, enabled scheduler, customer email or hosted write/deploy is performed in this task. Email presentation is authored only in `packages/ui/src/utils/appointmentReminderEmail.ts`. `node tooling/ui/build-appointment-reminder-email.mjs` produces its checked-in function-local deployment artifact; CI rejects drift and typechecks the isolated deployable function graph without parent-directory imports. This avoids pinned CLI parent-relative upload restrictions without introducing a second editable template. The new schedule tests mock only task-owned credential/cron/HTTP adapters and parse real extension signatures in full Supabase CI without executing them.
