Enrollment, contact consent, email verification, OS permission and caregiver grant are distinct facts; none imply another. Reuse the canonical shared settings/auth presentation and English/العربية behavior when approved. No patient UI or permission dialog is installed here.

## Recovery, withdrawal and privacy contract

Denied/revoked/disabled state explains the observed limitation and offers an intentional system-settings/recheck route. Returning to settings triggers one fresh capability/source/consent reconciliation, not replay of every historical event or repeated prompts. Unknown/delayed/dropped/acknowledged states stay distinct. No permission gain automatically changes communication consent or marks an alert delivered/taken.

Default proposed payload is neutral, with no medicine/dose/diagnosis/result/patient/doctor/caregiver name or chart URL on the lock screen. Private content needs separately approved privacy settings, fresh scope and a reviewed in-app destination; do not embed clinical data, bearer credentials or private object URLs. Legal privacy/retention review remains missing. The packet's proposed disclosure is not approval to ship clinical alerts.

Account/caregiver/claim/purpose/release/source withdrawal disables future eligible alerts and requires cancellation of owned obsolete notification IDs once the approved integration observes it. Server dispatch always rechecks fresh identity/relationship/purpose/source. An offline or force-stopped device cannot be promised immediate remote erasure or cancellation; zero private offline chart/notification cache is approved. A future reviewed design must bound capability/consent leases, handle local expiry/reboot and publish truthful residual limitations. Preserve action/consent provenance; do not delete clinical or patient-reported history to simulate revocation.
