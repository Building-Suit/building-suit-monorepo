## Proposed synthetic data and authority contract — unapproved

Keep patient identity, practice-patient relationship, encounter and location distinct. An encounter references the responsible doctor-owned practice, its existing patient relationship, an originating encounter location and time/version attribution. A location does not own a chart. Matching email/phone never merges identities, grants another doctor chart access or shares encounters. An independently shared secretary retains separate memberships and explicit grants for each doctor/location.

All proposed commands carry explicit practice, patient-relationship, encounter and location identifiers, expected version, idempotency key and typed entered payload. The server resolves the current verified product/environment Auth UID/SID, responsible doctor and approved actor capability; neither a form prop nor editable profile metadata supplies authority. Recheck current authority after waiting on write locks, including an otherwise identical receipt replay. The encounter ID/version and operation intent stay bound to that identity/scope. This is a conceptual review contract, not a created RPC/schema or an executed migration.

## Proposed encounter lifecycle — unapproved

The authoritative M2-002 objective names draft/in-progress/finalized/amended. The proposed mechanics preserve each source version; the qualified reviewer must approve permitted actors and the exact clinical meaning of each transition.

| Transition/action proposal | Preconditions and retained evidence | Reviewer decision needed |
|---|---|---|
| Create draft | Existing authorized practice-patient relationship, responsible doctor, originating location; actor/time/source provenance | Allowed initiator and required fields; no medical defaults proposed |
| Draft → in-progress | Current actor/scope/version and explicit command | Who may start and which staff actions remain drafts |
| Save draft/in-progress | Compare expected version and preserve one idempotent intent | Draft ownership, coauthor review and content conflict policy |
| In-progress → finalized | Explicit approved responsible-clinician action, current authority, complete approved reviewed payload | Clinical completeness/attestation and any signing distinction |
| Finalized → amended | Append a linked new version with original encounter/version, reason, actor and event time | Approved author/reason/review rules and visible amendment status |
| Further amendment | Append the next linked revision; retain all prior signed/final material | Limits, required review and release labeling |
| Withdraw/revoke final material | Preserve original bytes/content and append reason/status/actor/time; never erase history | Who can withdraw, legal meaning and how earlier release copies are handled |

Do not conflate administrative appointment `in_progress/completed` with clinical encounter finalization. No appointment lifecycle action supplies clinical sign-off or changes an encounter into a prescription.

Finalized/signed source material and its template/entered-data version must remain immutable. Archived patients and locations retain referenced history under appropriate current authorization. Retention duration, erasure and legal withdrawal consequences remain unresolved; this packet does not invent deletion or retention policy.

## Entered-data fidelity and provenance proposals — unapproved

Distinguish patient-reported, staff-entered, clinician-entered and explicitly clinician-reviewed content with source, actor, time, unit where relevant and revision. Keep unknown, none-reported and missing/uncollected as distinct states. Do not infer diagnoses, allergies, medicine identities, doses, units, routes or specialty completeness. ## Amendment, revocation, release and conflict proposals — unapproved

- **Corrections/amendments:** retain original immutable final content and append an attributed, reasoned, version-linked correction. Display review status without suggesting a previous signed copy was retroactively rewritten. Reviewer must approve who may draft/review/finalize amendments and the effect on already released material.
- **Membership/session revocation:** current UID/SID and actor capability are checked again after command lock waits and before accepting a receipt replay. Revoke A without changing independent B. Client scope loss clears clinical rows, open payloads, queries/listeners and late responses; it cannot silently select another doctor or reuse a draft for a new patient.
- **Release/print/delivery:** a permitted preview/print is not patient release or successful delivery. No public URLs, automated email/SMS/WhatsApp, patient portal/mobile assumption or provider action is introduced. Reviewer must identify release actor, patient/recipient confirmation, exact source version, inclusion/redaction, amendment/withdrawal labeling and failed/uncertain delivery handling. Provider/domain/legal gates remain separate.
- **Conflict/idempotency:** stale compare-and-swap versions fail explicitly without merging clinical text. Preserve a permitted actor's unsaved content through validation/network uncertainty; retry only unchanged intent with its original key. Do not blind-overwrite another contributor, sign an unreviewed latest version or relabel a timeout as a successful clinical action. Reviewer must approve collaboration and conflict-resolution behavior.
- **Audit/privacy:** append actual actor/time/action/source-version provenance and safe scoped projections. Do not expose raw clinical content, credentials, object paths or unrelated-practice events in administrative audit/search. Clinical audit access and legal retention remain separately reviewed.
