Use the canonical shared auth/signup/recovery/settings shell with patient-owned typed content and validation. English and العربية labels and theme behavior stay identical to the shared system. Collect only reviewed minimal account/challenge fields; government ID images, biometrics, contacts/camera/location permissions and default notification grants are not authorized. Public entry and claim failures disclose no relationship/diagnosis/chart existence. Exact patient email/OTP response policy requires the identity/privacy review; a clinician endpoint is not copied blindly.

After fresh server user/SID verification, an unclaimed account enters the reviewed claim flow, never doctor practice onboarding. Pending/disputed/expired/revoked relationships show no clinical data and cannot schedule medication reminders, delegate to caregivers or enable messaging. Enrollment grants no medication or communication consent. Each purpose begins unset and has its own explicit versioned consent and withdrawal history. The server must resolve the current account/relationship/purpose on every operation. Contact equality and recovery of an email account cannot rebind a disputed chart.

History views are limited to the current subject's scoped claim/consent/audit references and corrected provenance, with no reviewer proof payloads or another dependent/doctor's data. Patient clinical views must consume an explicitly released projection such as the existing limited-release presentation, not a read-only clinician workspace that still contains private working notes. A future claim never gives reciprocal doctor access.

Account/session/relationship changes, revocation, dispute, background/offline and policy expiry clear protected memory and suppress stale results. Zero private offline chart/draft cache, offline signing and queued reconnect business writes are approved. Device-loss handling cannot promise remote deletion while offline; server revocation and fresh admission remain required. Native notification/provider, legal retention, caregiver and reminder semantics need their own reviewed decisions.
