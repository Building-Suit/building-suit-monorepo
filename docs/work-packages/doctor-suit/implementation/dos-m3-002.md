## Contract

A request records an explicit patient relationship, consultation revision, clinic/name/timezone/version, requested local date/time window, and request-specific contact permission/source/server recording time. Permission starts off. This is synthetic administrative evidence, not marketing consent, guardian authority or clinical/legal approval. A changed consultation/clinic requires a newly reviewed request; no configuration update silently retimes the request.

Offer and acceptance run under the same practice calendar lock as ordinary booking. Buffered capacity includes effective offers and appointments through one interval rule and structural triggers. Reservations have an explicit expiry, expire before the appointment, and last at most one day; the duration is entered, not inferred. Existing offers remain immutable. Expired, cancelled, withdrawn-permission or archived-patient offers stop consuming capacity. Active effective offers block clinic archival. All business writes use Read Committed; direct table writes are unavailable.

Acceptance records an explicit operator reason/reference, releases the offer inside the same transaction, and invokes the authorized typed booking operation with its captured versions. Failure rolls back the reservation transition, appointment, audit and receipts together. One outer request key owns the complete transaction; its nested booking uses a private transaction-local key. Current scope, active patient and request permission are checked before acceptance receipt replay. Revoking contact does not erase an already accepted appointment, but prevents the old acceptance from being reused as current permission.

The states are waiting, offered, accepted, expired and cancelled. Read projections distinguish effective clock expiry from the explicit cleanup event. Requests/offers/events remain stored. No priority algorithm, clinical urgency, external message or provider acknowledgement is inferred. All responses report external_messages=disabled.

The new BsWaitlistPage owns presentation, canonical table/search/filter/paging, controlled request/offer/reason dialogs and history. Product orchestration owns business validation, permission adapters and commands. Dates, times, buffers and expiry remain literal and use explicit clinic timezone projections; record actions reuse shared pending, dirty-close, focus and uncertainty behavior. A cancelled availability read cannot leave the picker busy or overwrite a later editor.
