## Persisted contract

Collections/refunds belong to the doctor's practice, clinic, patient and immutable visit-charge source. They are separate from the SaaS subscription and manual InstaPay SaaS ledger. Exact positive minor units, currency and exponent bind to the selected current charge version. Fresh stored owner/session/admission authority is checked after the shared practice/subscriber locks; secretary patient/appointment grants confer no money authority. New preparations/dispatches require current clinic/entitlement write access. No price rounding, discount, real collection, tax invoice or automatic entitlement is inferred.

Immutable intents and append-only events preserve pending, unknown, failed and posted states with actor/time/reason attribution. Both pending and unknown collections reserve unallocated charge capacity; refunds reserve the exact original collection's remaining refundable balance. Refunds cannot be refunded again as if they were a collection. Atomic reconciliation posts an immutable ledger entry, its exact charge-version allocation and one scoped synthetic receipt number in one transaction. Counter scope cannot change or be deleted/reset. Failed or unattempted-cancelled requests consume no receipt number. Later corrections retain original amounts/entries and cannot reduce a charge below net posted plus reserved collection amounts. Cancelling an appointment never erases a charge or invents a refund; new collections on cancelled/no-show or changed-patient visits require review. Refunds retain the original financial patient/source.

The synthetic dispatcher persists accepted/rejected transport evidence separately from ledger posting and acknowledgement. An attempted outcome remains unknown until explicit reconciliation reads that evidence; it cannot be blindly resent under a new key, cancelled to free its reservation, marked paid from an HTTP reply or rejected without evidence. Original-key command receipts acknowledge historical operations and never grant current authority. An already accepted result can be reconciled after clinic/entitlement writes become unavailable: this records the earlier outcome and initiates no movement or entitlement. No external money transport/provider account exists or is activated; every projection explicitly reports external transport/live collection/tax invoice false.
