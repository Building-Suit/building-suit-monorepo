# DOS-AE-003 administrative allocation quotas

This child follows PR374's explicitly accepted trial periods. It enforces clinic and staff allocation against the practice owner's original subscribed terms. It does not complete DOS-AE-003: general business-write entitlement guards, context/cache/UI binding and aggregate provider acceptance follow separately. No offer, subscription, payment route, operator approval or live actor is seeded or enabled. Original clinical dependencies remain unchanged.

Outside synthetic preview, a new clinic, clinic reactivation, active staff membership/reactivation or pending invitation/reopening/extension requires the owning practice's active period. A recipient with an independent Doctor subscription cannot use it to fund another practice. Ownership comes from the stored practice, never browser metadata. Explicit limited zero means zero; only approved explicit unlimited terms remove a cap. Replacing the current offer does not rewrite a subscriber's original allowance.

Staff usage includes active memberships and pending, unexpired invitations. Invitation acceptance creates a membership before marking the invitation accepted; the final transaction check consumes that reservation without double counting. Deferred constraint triggers acquire the owner's financial advisory lock, refresh provider identity, admission, period state and database time, then count final rows using fresh read-committed snapshots. A competing commit is included after waiting. Other transaction isolation levels fail closed for allocation because their retained snapshot cannot supply this guarantee. No business/FK row writes follow the deferred lock acquisition.

Normal commands retain existing authorization, domain locks, versions, idempotency receipts and audit. A failed final quota check rolls back the entire command, including new onboarding profile/practice, records, receipt and success audit. Successful exact receipt replay adds no allocation. The physical-table checks also cover future privileged command paths; their helpers remain unavailable to browser and service API roles.

Suspension/revocation of staff, invitation cancellation and unused-clinic archival remain available while a subscription is held, expired or over limit. Rows, grants, subscribed periods and historical evidence are retained. Reactivation is an increase and requires current authority. No automatic deletion, archival, trial reset or grant rewriting occurs. Owner status adds own-practice usage without exposing another owner's account. DS002/DS003/DS006 map to bounded staff-limit, clinic-limit and inactive application errors without backend detail. These quota checks do not yet constitute complete authorization for all administrative business writes.
