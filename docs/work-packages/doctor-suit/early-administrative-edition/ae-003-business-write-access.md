# DOS-AE-003 administrative business write access

This child follows PR375. It checks current subscription authority for administrative business writes while retaining authorized history and security reductions. Context/cache/UI binding and final actual-provider acceptance remain separate work; DOS-AE-003 and original clinical dependencies are not marked complete.

Outside synthetic preview, physical guards cover Doctor profiles, practices, clinics, memberships and location grants, secretary invitations, patient identities and relationships, schedules and appointments. Existing command authorization, tenant/location checks, domain locks, versions, receipts and audit remain in place. Before writes, guards check the practice owner's original subscribed terms and current period. Deferred checks acquire the same subscriber lock as financial state changes, refresh provider identity, admission, period state and database time, then validate the committed allowance using read-committed snapshots. No business rows are written after that final lock. A secretary or independently subscribed Doctor cannot substitute their own subscription for the practice owner's account.

The two explicit over-limit policies have distinct behavior. `block_new_allocation` preserves ordinary edits while denying additional allocation above any cap. `read_only_until_within_limit` blocks ordinary business writes until usage is reduced. Missing, scheduled, expired and suspended periods deny writes. These checks grant no role, clinical authority, document access, paid period or live admission. Trusted terms remain unconfigured by default, and existing immutable periods are never extended or reset.

Security reductions remain available: staff suspension/revocation, narrower location/capability grants, invitation cancellation and unused-clinic archival. Grant commands apply updates/deletions only to changed existing rows and insert only new location grants; they do not implement a reduction by deleting/reinserting the entire set. This preserves reductions for legacy inherited grants and keeps unchanged independent memberships intact. Reopening or expanding access requires current authority. An archival reduction cannot be used to rewrite an already archived timestamp during a hold.

New patient identities must link to their practice relationship by commit. A failure after waiting rolls back the complete patient operation, including identity, relationship, correction version, activity, receipt and audit. Orphan identities and identity-history updates fail closed. An exact committed receipt can be replayed without granting a new write. Authorized patient/history reads and personal account/recovery settings remain available when a period expires or is held; revoked role grants remain revoked. This layer does not create privacy/export request APIs or exempt future ordinary business commands from enforcement.

Seven new real transaction scenarios plus all 56 inherited cases pass: new-patient and correction rollback after an owner hold, cross-owner funding denial, independent subscriber progress, admission/session revocation after waiting, and an immutable period actually expiring while commit waits. Tests verify no orphan identity, receipt or activity survives a denied write. Both over-limit policies, recovery by reducing usage, schedule insert/update, appointment edits, historical expiry reads, personal settings, legacy grant reductions and permission expansions are covered. Root units pass (1,870 with 23 explicit optional skips), nine focused SaaS units pass, Doctor/Edge types, lint, strict boundaries and local security advisors pass. Actual pinned PGMeta generation leaves the public type shape unchanged.

All fixtures are disposable and synthetic. This SQL runner provides minimal provider interfaces; actual administrative-candidate GoTrue/Storage/Edge/Realtime acceptance remains required for the aggregate, along with subscription state in context/cache/UI and parent review. Original planning files and task statuses are preserved. No hosted operation, deployment, credential change, Automation action, main/staging merge, genuine commercial offer, merchant route or live approval is included.

Under `read_only_until_within_limit`, that temporary count incorrectly rejected acceptance exactly at the staff cap (`DS007`). Applied migrations remain unchanged.

All 63 inherited real transaction cases and three new concurrent reservation cases pass: same-key acceptance retries, two reserved invitations accepted at their cap, and acceptance versus an additional allocation. The rejected allocation retains no invitation, receipt or audit event; successful conversion retains its accepted audit exactly once.
