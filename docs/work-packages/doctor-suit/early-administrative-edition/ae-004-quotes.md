## Contract

`doctor_create_saas_quote(p_terms, p_configuration_revision, p_account_revision, p_interval, p_document_ref, p_key)` derives the subscriber from a fresh verified Doctor session and resolves the optional practice from stored ownership. An owner can prepare an offer before clinic onboarding. Delegated-only identities cannot enroll themselves. Current approved immutable terms and their exact bilingual document must exist. Monthly/yearly prices, currency and exponent come exclusively from that version; missing interval prices are unavailable, including when another interval is zero. The caller supplies no total, identity, currency, provider, callback or return URL. PostgREST argument matching rejects additional parameters; the app adapter also rejects additional request fields before transport.

A quote records the selected offer configuration revision, current subscription revision/state/period and practice association. Integer minor units are returned as decimal strings; there is no floating-point calculation, currency conversion, inferred credit, proration, discount, tax treatment or invoice total. `configured_saas_offer` means the explicit base offer for the selected interval. It does not decide billing dates or whether a new purchase, renewal or plan change is legally/commercially available. Those decisions remain pending. No arbitrary expiry duration or commercial policy is invented: fresh intent preparation instead validates the recorded offer and current subscription/practice state, including time-derived trial expiry.

`doctor_saas_quote(p_quote)` reads only the authenticated subscriber's immutable historical quote. A historical quote may be stale and cannot be treated as a checkout authorization. `doctor_prepare_saas_payment(p_quote, p_key)` checks the same owner, locks that subscriber, and rejects changed configuration, account revision, period, state or practice. Preparation creates at most one immutable intent per quote, with state `awaiting_route_approval`. Exact retries return the historical receipt after fresh session/admission/owner checks; a receipt is never live authority. New preparation against a stale quote fails, including when an earlier held intent exists. Failed commands leave no partial intent or receipt. Actor/subscriber locks use onboarding-compatible order, and fresh checks run after advisory and configuration row-lock waits. Write RPCs require read-committed isolation so stale snapshots cannot conceal revocation.

Both new public tables force RLS and revoke all raw privileges from anonymous, authenticated and service roles. Public RPC wrappers invoke narrowly granted private implementations with an empty search path. Raw records and command receipts retain actor attribution; immutable triggers prevent rewriting/deleting/truncating quote and intent history. Types are generated from the migrated disposable PostgreSQL using pinned PGMeta, not manually patched.

## Closed payment route and acceptance boundaries

Every quote/intent response declares `payment_route.state=unconfigured`; merchant eligibility, route approval, credentials custody and SaaS tax-document applicability are `pending`. Provider acceptance is `unrun`. Checkout URL is null, and purchase, paid activation and live activation are false. There is no route setter, provider adapter, callback handler, credential, charge, paid period or entitlement transition. An intent cannot be marked paid, and zero-priced synthetic evidence cannot grant access. These deliberately closed interfaces can support separately reviewed route implementation after the operator decisions exist.

- **C01:** exact approved version/interval/identity/subscription binding; owner-only historical reads; stale configuration/account/hold rejection; exact quote retry, immutable money and one held intent per quote.
- **C02:** route and approval fields remain closed and are validated by the app decoder. AE-D02/D03/D04 remain pending, including commercial policy, merchant eligibility, costs/refunds, custody and qualified current tax applicability.
- **C03:** pgTAP, transaction and adapter tests are synthetic code evidence only. They are not provider/merchant acceptance. No account, credential, charge or callback activation occurs.
- **C04:** no clinic service prices, patient charges/collections, receipts, tax submission, clinical content or new UI is added. Existing components/templates remain in `packages/ui` and apps own orchestration.
