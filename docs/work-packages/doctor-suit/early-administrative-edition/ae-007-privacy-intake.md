# DOS-AE-007 — own-account privacy review intake

This bounded synthetic slice records a signed-in account holder’s access/correction/erasure/restriction review request. The immutable receipt attributes the verified actor, session and database actor. It accepts no free text, patient identifier, selected tenant, clinical information or export destination. One unresolved request per coded kind bounds both the inbox and read projection at four rows; same-key replay is stable and a changed kind conflicts. Account identity, not practice ownership or staff grants, controls the scope.

Own-only forced RLS and narrowly granted invoker/private-definer RPCs protect reads and writes. Direct client writes and service-client access are revoked. The command checks actor/admission before and after its actor lock. Unconfigured, expired and held subscriptions do not prevent an admitted account from recording or reading its own request. Candidate admission and valid sessions still apply; this is not a public patient rights portal.

The shared `BsPrivacyRequestsPage` owns the complete coded form and request table. Doctor supplies bilingual copy, identity-bound repository/controller state and commands. Unknown committed responses retain their command key; identity changes and explicit clears reject stale work. No receipt grants business authority. Existing shared sign-in, brand and token sources remain unchanged.

Validation: 35 new SQL assertions plus 1,044 inherited assertions (1,079 total), and all 66 inherited transaction scenarios pass. The root regression passes 1,906 units with 23 expected infrastructure skips; five focused controller/decoder tests cover retry, duplicate clicks, exact identity and cancellation. CLI-pinned PostgreSQL Meta generated matching app/Edge types from the actual migrated disposable schema. Doctor types, Edge types, lint, build and strict ownership checks cover this change. Both EN desktop/light and AR mobile/dark browser journeys pass, including abort-after-commit retry and independent-account isolation. Disposable CLI security advisors pass at warn/error level.

Qualified privacy/controller-processor policy, verified outcome adjudication, patient/caregiver identity, retention, holds, deadlines and accountable fulfillment remain unconfigured. The page explicitly says no request is transmitted to an external reviewer and no export/correction/erasure occurs. This slice does not complete DOS-M7-004 or grant legal approval, automated erasure, paid/live activation or real-data admission. Original clinical PR366 remains held. No hosted operation, deployment, credential change, Automation activation or protected-branch merge occurs here.
