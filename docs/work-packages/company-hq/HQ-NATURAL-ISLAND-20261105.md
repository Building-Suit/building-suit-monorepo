# HQ natural island landmass — 5 November 2026

The founder requested a natural island instead of a manufactured platform, limited strictly to the original HQ landmass and its surface materials. This terrain treatment supersedes the smooth top and regular cliff treatment in the earlier [island lighting record](HQ-ISLAND-LIGHTING-20261105.md). The [circular workplace layout](HQ-CIRCULAR-WORKPLACES-20261105.md) remains intact. Earlier dated records and accepted release fragments remain historical evidence.

`naturalIslandGeometry` supplies the land top, weathered cliffs, sparse coastal rocks and tiny grass tufts. Deterministic erosion fields recess the upper coastline within the existing island envelope, forming unequal bays, broken edges and varying ledges. Seven cliff sample layers vary their setbacks and elevations independently; alternating facets break up repeated strata without introducing mountains. Exposed coastal relief is shallow and bounded. The protected disk through the outer workplace floor plus 0.12 scene units stays exactly at the existing ground height, preserving flat support beneath the Orb, workplaces and circulation floor. Decorations remain outside that protected area.

The existing footprint remains the authority for the camera envelope and surrounding water. A lower rocky shelf overlaps the existing water's inner edge at its unchanged sea level, so the recessed upper coast has rock beneath it without changing the water mesh or shader. Gold coast accents follow the reshaped land; their palette and existing lighting remain unchanged. The terrain keeps its closed underside and matte treatment, with no ground reflection pass.

`naturalIslandMaterial` adds a separate cached 1024² RGBA data texture for land only. Warped fields encode shallow normal slopes, weathered mineral variation, pores, interrupted fractures, soil and sparse moss pockets. World-space sampling blends the existing dark Coal/Navy aesthetic into muted earthy and moss variation with broad rough response. It avoids metallic highlights and regular contour bands. Generation happens once on the CPU, with cached pixels reused on context restoration; mipmaps use about 5.33 MiB of GPU texture storage, an allocation estimate rather than a performance measurement. The renderer owns texture cleanup on failure and disposal.

The original `hqIslandGeometry` footprint helper and 512² `hologramTerrainMaterial` remain unchanged. The new land material uses texture unit 2 and a dedicated land shader branch, leaving structural concrete and workplace materials on their existing sampler. Both new utilities have explicit shared-UI package exports. The central Orb, all offices, furniture, shared circular floor and partitions, existing roads, lighting, water, camera, controls, selection/focus behavior and page layout are outside this task and retain their existing contracts. No department, company-plan, Auth, Brain, Realtime or provider behavior changes.

Work remains in the primary checkout on `codex/company-hq/office-design`, under draft PR #528. Only source inspection was performed for this terrain change. No tests, builds, lint, typechecks, boundary/release checks, browser/API/runtime checks, benchmarks or tester/reviewer agents were run, following the standing founder instruction. Visual fidelity, surface readability and performance remain unmeasured. No merge, deployment, production action or public product activation is recorded.
