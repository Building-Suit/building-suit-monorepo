## Apply to the still-image mockup

- Preserve the supplied B geometry, proportions, windows, doorway and rendering; no invented skyline mark, plain-letter B, recoloring, flipping, cropping, extra shadow/glow or flattening.
- Place the light/silver-and-gold master on a calm dark field, with clear space; minimum 40px high digitally, preferably 64px or more. Logo fidelity in generated artwork must be visually checked; generation does not establish pixel-perfect asset compositing.
- Building Navy `#16293B`; Deep Structure Navy `#0D1B28`; Premium Gold `#D89B42`; Highlight Gold `#EBB45A`. Dark UI background `#0E1114`, surface `#151A1F`, text `#F5F6F7`.
- English typography: Manrope. Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Preserve **Building Suit**, not “Building Suite.” Serif signage and the invented skyline icon in earlier drafts are not the documented identity.
- Restrain gold emphasis; the source design system allows one large gold focal element per screen. Do not treat gold decoration as proof of task status.

The output is a proposed design image. Exact unaltered logo file placement and font embedding in a future website must be checked after the founder authorizes implementation.
