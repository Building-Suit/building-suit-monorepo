# HQ reference island and softened office lighting — 5 November 2026

The founder requested readable offices with standing lights and Brain light, then softened the lighting and supplied a specific island reference. The latest request replaces uniform circular growth: new land must extend only on the side that needs another workplace. The reference is implemented as procedural 3D geometry, not a bitmap background. Work remains in the primary checkout and PR #528.

Shared `officeArchitecture` adds a rear-perimeter standing lamp to every department office and the CEO workspace. Its shade, warm emitter, stem and base use existing material batches; maximum height 1.815 stays below the existing 1.9 room picking volume. Pale Silver/Pearl/Gold surfaces remain alongside Coal seating and Navy cabinetry. Room power is softened to 0.80 and Brain power to `0.86 + reveal × 0.26`, with bloom strength 0.48. These are unshadowed raster lights and bounded bloom effects, without per-office shadow maps or hardware ray tracing.

Shared `createHqIslandFootprint` returns the exact coast used by the renderer and minimap. A fixed initial first-ring island is combined with independent base-connected envelopes of actual buffered office rectangles and road capsules. The radial union is sampled at 256 fixed world angles; conservative local smoothing only raises the required boundary and spreads over four neighbouring samples. Adding an ordinary later office therefore extends its own sector without scaling the opposite coast. The circular radius is a camera envelope, not the visible island shape. No office, road or approved record is moved to fit the coast.

The reference treatment uses a broad, flat Coal/Navy stone top, a thick faceted rim, tapered rock layers and a closed underside. Fine Premium Gold seams trace the upper coast and a middle rock layer. Fixed 2.25-unit sculpted depth keeps existing terrain unchanged as new land is added; the bottom extends to 1.12 times that depth. A procedural alpha-gradient ground shadow uses one geometry batch without a new render target. At most eight sparse clusters of neutral rocks and tiny muted straw-coloured grass are placed away from offices, roads, the hub and coastal drops. No characters or employee assignments are added.

The latest material request adds a real cached procedural 512² RGBA data texture. It encodes shallow normal slopes, broad organic stone/terrace fields and fine rough grain; palette colours still come from canonical Coal/Navy/Gold values. Terrain and sparse rocks use world-space material coordinates with linear/trilinear mipmaps. Generation happens once, with cached CPU pixels reused after context restoration; the GPU material is released on renderer disposal or initialization failure. No external photograph, bitmap backdrop or per-fragment octave-noise loop is used. Two texture reads shade each terrain fragment.

The terrain is matte: the earlier mirrored geometry pass, floor reflection sampler and reflection framebuffer/depth targets are removed completely. Bloom alone retains the native scene-copy/bright-pass/two-blur composition. Its worst-case texture source budget is approximately 36 MB, plus 1.33 MiB for the material with mipmaps, excluding canvas/geometry/driver overhead. This is a bounded allocation estimate, not measured memory/performance.

The latest water request adds a 256-angle/eight-band procedural sea annulus following the actual coast. It sits 0.5 units below the walkable top, tucks beneath the upper rock edge, fades offshore at 2.4 times the island envelope and shades soft Navy wave highlights with thin Pearl shore foam. Water has no mirrored island/object reflection; the terrain stays matte and bloom-only. Office/road placement, land hit volumes and minimap coast do not change. Its mesh rebuilds with occupied-sector layout changes, not per animation frame.

Meshes are rebuilt only when the actual office layout changes and old GPU buffers are released. Pan, orbit and ordinary animation allocate no island geometry. Existing camera-above-ground constraints, room picking, reduced-motion behavior and pixel/effect budgets remain. The minimap terrain polygon and visible-ground clip use the same shared coast. Ordinary newer approvals append under the existing approved-date/ID sort; backdated imports or insertion inside equal-date legacy batches can reorder slots, so persistent placement for those administrative cases would need an explicit source contract. This task does not invent one.
