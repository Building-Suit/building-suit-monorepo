The scene uses native WebGL without a new rendering dependency. It constructs a shaded circular pedestal, concentric illuminated rings, a translucent floating sphere, orbit rings, a light projection and suspended particles from local geometry. Motion pauses offscreen/in hidden tabs and respects reduced-motion preferences. WebGL buffers, shaders and the program are disposed with the scene; the interface provides a localized unavailable state when the renderer cannot start. The app route retains the strict shared-template boundary; no app-local presentation, CSS or Vue component layer is introduced. Camera state is local to the mounted workspace and does not introduce accounts, persistence or backend activity. Historical migrations, private ignored data and earlier design records remain preserved.

## Controls

Left mouse drag or one-finger touch drag rotates the view. Right/middle mouse drag or Shift + drag moves the view. The mouse wheel and touch pinch change zoom; a two-finger drag moves the view. The first two overview buttons zoom out/in. The third button resets camera rotation, pan offsets and zoom together to the centered default view at 100%.

The focused workspace also accepts arrow keys to rotate, Shift + arrow keys to move, +/− to zoom, and Home or 0 to restore defaults. Header navigation and business actions remain disabled and the status does not claim an active connection or agent activity.
